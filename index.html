Chapter 57: Bedtime Stories - The Traitor And A Debt

Written by Jason Jonation

Of Judgement Days And “The Rages Band” • April 8, 2020

Chapter 1: Introduction To The Poverty And Debts In Town of Norseville

Once upon a time in the town of Norseville, the strict businessmen and government officials demanded ruthless tax debts. When workers could not pay the exorbitant monthly demands of $1,000,000, they were plunged into poverty. Enter Narcisco, a diligent worker who wielded magical spells using the sacred words "Lusla Elganio" to manifest gold and save the innocent.

Chapter 2 & 3: Narcisco's Challenge & Summons of the Humble Army

Branded a "traitor" by the corrupt government for teaching people how to live without exorbitant debt, Narcisco stood firm against their tanks and enforcers. Using humble magic, he summoned troops and good governments to defeat the greedy regime and restore balance to Norseville.

Moral Lesson

"Be kind and gentle to the poorest people. Be humble to give whatever they are the most poorest... Your money is not your friend hence it is only a liability to defeat poverty. Be contented with what you have."
